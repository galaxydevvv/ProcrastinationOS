Welcome to the desktop!

This simple help guide will show you around and help you get started.

Step 1: The Taskbar

This is where all of your open and pinned apps will appear.

Your clock will also be displayed here along with the date.

Step 2: "Productivity" Features

We have tons of very important features to help you work effectively!

For example;
  • Doomscroll locking. This makes it so when you doomscroll it's hard to stop so you don't miss any good videos!

  • Focus Timer to help you stay on track, 5 minuites of work no limit of games!

  • While you are working little events will occur to keep you "engaged" on your work!

  • So much more!!
